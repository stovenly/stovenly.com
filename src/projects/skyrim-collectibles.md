A browser tool that reads a Skyrim save and shows which collectibles are still missing.

## Features

- Supports Skyrim SE, Skyrim VR and Skyrim (2011) save files (.ess)
- Missing items as a list and as map pins
- Tracks all collectibles:
  - Dragon Priest Masks
  - Stones of Barenziah
  - Daedric Artifacts
  - Dragon Claws
  - Black Books
  - Words of Power
  - Paragons
  - Pages of Jiub's Opus
  - Kagrumez Resonance Gems
  - Insects in Jars
  - Thieves Guild Trophies
